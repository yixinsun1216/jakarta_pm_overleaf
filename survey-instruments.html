Survey Instruments

Baseline Survey Instruments

Study participants were asked the following survey questions during a baseline survey, before the installation of the air quality monitoring hardware.1These questions were part of a longer survey. Bahasa Indonesian translations are in parentheses.

Note that because the hardware was deployed as part of a randomized trial in which treated households received the same fan unit fitted with a filter, the survey instruments refer to it as an ‘air purifier’, but all households analyzed in this paper are control households, whose unit had no filter (“fan”).

  1. What was your household’s total income in the past month (in IDR)? This includes cash transfers from the government (Berapa penghasilan rumah tangga dalam sebulan terakhir? (dalam Rupiah))

    1. \(<\)Rp 1.000.000

    2. Rp 1.000.000 - Rp 2.999.999

    3. Rp 3.000.000 - Rp 4.999.999

    4. Rp 5.000.000-Rp 7.999.999

    5. Rp 8.000.000 - Rp 9.999.999

    6. Rp 10.000.000 - Rp12.999.999

    7. \(>\)13.000.000

    8. Does not know (Tidak tahu)

    9. Refuse to answer (Menolak menjawab)

  2. How many rooms does this residence have? (Berapa banyak kamar yang dimiliki hunian ini?)

    [Enumerator: Count spaces as separate rooms only if they have a formal entrance, even if the door is a curtain. Do not count one room divided by curtains without a formal entrance as separate rooms. Family-owned shops attached to the residence and bathrooms should count as rooms.]
    ([Enumerator: ruangan dianggap terpisah jika ada tempat masuk, meskipun pintunya adalah kain. Jika tidak ada tempat masuk, meski terpisah oleh kain/gorden, jangan dihitung sebagai ruangan terpisah. Toko yang bersatu dengan rumah, kamar mandi, dihitung sebagai satu ruangan terpisah.])

  3. In the past 7 days, how do you think air quality was in your home, on the scale we mentioned earlier? (Dalam seminggu terakhir, berdasarkan skala yang sudah saya sebutkan tadi, menurut Ibu/Bapak seberapa baik kualitas udara di Jakarta?)

    1. Good (green) (Baik (hijau))

    2. Moderate (yellow) (Sedang (kuning))

    3. Unhealthy for sensitive groups (orange) (Tidak sehat untuk kelompok rentan (oranye))

    4. Unhealthy (red) (Tidak sehat (merah))

    5. Very unhealthy (purple) (Sangat tidak sehat (ungu))

    6. Hazardous (Brown) (Berbahaya (coklat))

    7. Doesn’t know (Tidak tahu)

  4. Is there an air conditioner in the house? (Apakah terdapat AC/pendingin udara di dalam rumah?)

    1. No (Tidak)

    2. Yes, in room the hardware is in (Ya, di ruangan yang terdapat air purifier dan air monitor)

    3. Yes, in another room in the house (Ya, di ruangan lain)

  5. In the past 7 days, how many times did you see trash being burned in your neighborhood? (Dalam seminggu terakhir, berapa kali Ibu/Bapak melihat sampah dibakar di lingkungan Ibu/Bapak?)

    1. Never (Tidak pernah)

    2. 1 or 2 times (1-2 kali)

    3. 3 or more times (3 kali atau lebih)

    4. Does not know (Tidak tahu)

    5. Refuse to answer (Menolak menjawab)

  6. What is the main cooking fuel you use in your home? (Apa bahan bakar utama untuk memasak yang Ibu/Bapak gunakan di rumah?)

    1. Electric (Kompor listrik)

    2. Gas/LPG (LPG)

    3. Kerosene (Minyak tanah)

    4. Charcoal/briquettes/coconut shells (Arang/briket/batok kelapa)

    5. Firewood (Kayu bakar)

    6. Other (Lainnya)

  7. I have a list of activities that could cause air pollution in the room the hardware is set up in. Can you verify if any of the following occur in the room of the purifier? (Saya memiliki daftar aktivitas yang dapat menyebabkan polusi udara di ruangan tempat alat dipasang. Bisakah Ibu/Bapak memberitahu apakah terdapat aktivitas berikut yang terjadi di ruangan yang terdapat air purifier?)

    1. Gaps in doors and windows (Celah di pintu dan udara)

    2. Doors/windows are always open (Pintu/jendela yang selalu terbuka)

    3. Close to kitchen (Dekat dengan dapur)

    4. Smoking/vaping in or near the room (Merokok/vaping di dalam/dekat ruangan)

    5. Whether the room has doors and is connected to another room (Ruangan memiliki pintu yang terhubung dengan ruangan lain)

    6. Garbage burning in the neighborhood (Pembakaran sampah di lingkungan sekitar)

    7. Candles/Incense (Difuser/Pelembab udara/humidifier)

    8. None

The baseline survey included a time-use recall module covering the period from 4 AM the previous day to 10 AM on the interview day. For each contiguous activity block, the respondent identified the activity from a fixed set of mutually exclusive categories and reported the hour at which the block ended. The cooking indicator used in our main specification (1[CookingHour] in Equation 1) is constructed from the adult respondent’s reported cooking hours.

  1. We now want to ask you about your activities from 4 AM the previous day to 10 AM today, the interview day. An activity is anything you did during the day. Activities include both active tasks like socializing, preparing food, or eating, and quieter tasks like relaxing and sleeping. These questions are for research purposes only; if you do not feel comfortable, you are not required to answer. If you are comfortable, I will begin with your activity at 4 AM yesterday. (Pada bagian ini, saya akan menanyakan aktivitas Ibu/Bapak sejak pukul 4 pagi kemarin hingga pukul 10 pagi hari ini. Aktivitas yang dimaksud merupakan apapun yang Ibu/Bapak lakukan sepanjang hari yang mencakup bersosialisasi, menyiapkan makanan, makan, bersantai, tidur, bekerja, dan hal lainnya. Pertanyaan-pertanyaan ini dimaksudkan semata-mata untuk tujuan penelitian, tetapi jika Ibu/Bapak merasa tidak nyaman untuk menjawab pertanyaan ini, Ibu/Bapak diperbolehkan untuk tidak menjawab.)

  2. Repeated for each activity block, beginning at 4 AM and continuing until the cumulative time reaches 24 hours:

    At [previous block end time], what activity were you doing? Please select from the following: (Pada pukul [previous block end time], aktivitas apa yang Ibu/Bapak lakukan? Pilih aktivitas berikut:)

    1. Inside own home — awake (Di dalam rumah Ibu/Bapak sendiri — Bangun/Terjaga)

    2. Inside own home — asleep (Di dalam rumah Ibu/Bapak sendiri — Tidur)

    3. Inside own home — cooking (Di dalam rumah Ibu/Bapak sendiri — Memasak)

    4. Work / school (Bekerja / sekolah)

    5. Outdoors, non-exercise (Di luar ruangan — tidak ada hubungannya dengan olah raga)

    6. Commuting (Bepergian)

    7. Outside the home, in an indoor space (Di luar rumah, di ruang dalam ruangan)

  3. What hour did you end this activity? (Pada pukul berapa Ibu/Bapak mengakhiri aktivitas ini?)

    ([Enumerator: catat jam berakhir dengan format 24 jam, misalnya pukul 1 siang dicatat sebagai 13. Modul akan terus diulang menanyakan aktivitas berikutnya hingga total waktu mencapai 24 jam. Sistem akan memberi peringatan jika total kurang dari 24 atau lebih dari 31 jam.])

Endline Survey Instruments

Study participants were asked the following survey questions during an endline survey, after the air quality monitoring hardware had been installed for two to three months.2These questions were part of a longer survey. Bahasa Indonesian translations are in parentheses.

  1. In the last 24 hours, did someone smoke inside your home? (Dalam 24 jam terakhir, apakah seseorang merokok di dalam rumah Anda?)

    1. Yes (Ya)

    2. No (Tidak)

    3. Does not know (Tidak tahu)

    4. Refuse to answer (Menolak menjawab)

Whatsapp Survey

Study participants were also asked high frequency questions via Whatsapp during the course of the study. In particular, they were asked about their window/door opening activities:

  1. In the last hour, was the door to the room where the air quality equipment is stored open to the outside? (Dalam 1 jam terakhir, apakah pintu di ruangan tempat menyimpan alat kualitas udara terbuka ke luar? (jika ada pintu yang terbuka ke luar ruangan))

    1. Yes (Ya)

    2. No (Tidak)

    3. There are no outside doors (Tiada pintu ke luar)

  2. In the last hour, was the window in the room where the air quality equipment is stored open to the outside? (Dalam 1 jam terakhir, apakah jendela di ruangan tempat menyimpan alat kualitas udara terbuka ke luar? (jika ada jendela yang terbuka ke luar ruangan))

    1. Yes (Ya)

    2. No (Tidak)

    3. There are no outside windows (Tak ada jendela luar)
